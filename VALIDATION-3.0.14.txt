Remnawave Reverse 3.0.14 validation
Date: 2026-08-09 UTC

Fix target:
- Installer could stop immediately after "Docker Engine and Docker Compose are ready" when apt-daily/unattended-upgrades acquired dpkg locks asynchronously.

Changes:
- SCRIPT_VERSION=3.0.14.
- Added wait_for_apt_ready() for dpkg/apt lock files with a 15-minute timeout and diagnostics.
- Added apt_get_safe() with DPkg::Lock::Timeout=900.
- All bootstrap APT operations now wait for transient apt/dpkg locks.
- After Docker becomes ready the installer explicitly waits for any background APT job and then continues automatically.
- unattended-upgrades configuration is idempotent and retried instead of aborting on a transient lock.
- Added RU/EN progress messages so the installer no longer appears to silently stop after Docker installation.
